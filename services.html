Preventive Care

Preventive Dentistry

Preventive dentistry focuses on maintaining your oral health through regular check-ups and proactive care. By identifying potential concerns early, preventive care can help you avoid more complex treatments down the road.

  • Comprehensive dental examinations
  • Professional teeth cleaning (prophylaxis)
  • Digital dental X-rays
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Dental sealants
  • Personalized oral hygiene guidance

Cosmetic Enhancement

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening can help address discoloration caused by everyday factors like coffee, tea, and aging. Our whitening services use carefully applied techniques designed to brighten your smile while prioritizing the health of your enamel.

  • In-office professional whitening treatments
  • Custom take-home whitening kits
  • Pre-treatment sensitivity assessment
  • Shade consultation and goal-setting
  • Post-treatment care instructions

Aesthetic Options

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry encompasses a range of aesthetic procedures designed to enhance the appearance of your smile. From subtle improvements to more comprehensive makeovers, options are tailored to your individual goals and dental health.

  • Porcelain and composite veneers
  • Dental bonding
  • Smile design consultations
  • Tooth reshaping and contouring
  • Gum contouring

Rebuilding Smiles

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing and replacing damaged or missing teeth. Using modern materials and techniques, restorative treatments aim to restore both the function and appearance of your natural teeth.

  • Tooth-colored composite fillings
  • Dental crowns and bridges
  • Dental implant consultations
  • Root canal therapy
  • Dentures and partial dentures
  • Inlays and onlays

Personalized Guidance

Dental Consultations

A dental consultation provides the opportunity to discuss your oral health goals, ask questions, and explore treatment options in a no-pressure environment. Consultations are the first step toward understanding what care may be right for you.

  • Initial oral health assessment
  • Treatment option discussion
  • Second opinion consultations
  • Cosmetic consultation and smile preview
  • Oral health education

  • Pediatric dental examinations
